Session 25
============

2026 08 23

So we're being asked to join a coup against the local government and align ourselves against a powerful figure who clearly has supernatural powers.

BH asks what her policy is on the Vistani. She doesn't trust them, mostly based on their connection to Strahd. We also get the sense that she thinks of them as commoners.

Charity graciously accepts that the Lady requests help from the Flowerhope family and asks where the Lady sees herself in five years. The Lady's answer broadly covers that her bloodline would do a better job of running Vallaki -- the scale of her ambition seems to be focussed on this town.

We decide we're in. Her timeline is vague but she assures us that if we take Issac out, she'll be ready. We're not so sure but we don't think Strahd would let a properly competent ruler take over a town, so the Lady is probably as good as anyone.

We decide to pursue information as our payment, and ask about the mountains to the south. She's not got much to offer, aside from rumours of treasure, weird cults, people going missing, and the secret to defeating Strahd.

We fill out our map, arrange our way to contact her again when we need to (talk to Ernst at the organisation's soup kitchen) and depart.

We walk back to the Bluewater, but on the way there we run into Gastonovich and some cronies, bleating about how we cheated him.

He right, but Aryn protests that we didn't cheat at all (and calls him "Gastono*bitch*"). She doesn't convince G but some of his cronies start to get worried that maybe a beat-down isn't the right thing to do.

There's a bit more chat and some veiled threats but then Charity tosses a Tasha's Hideous Laughter onto G and he sinks to the ground laughing. We tell his companions that it seems like G had too much to drink and they should get him home before he hurts himself, and head off down the road.

One of the braver companions calls for us to stop, but a very heavy nudge to his shoulder from Banhammer helps him to reconsider and also knocks him over onto still-laughing Gastonovich.

The rest of the cronies have lost their nerve and we walk off with no further interruption. We arrive at the Bluewater, greeted by a sleepy but slightly worried-looking Danica as we enter. She locks the door behind us and we all head to our bedrooms; we spend a few minutes talking about our plan for tomorrow before hitting the bunks.

After breakfast Charity wants to check in on Sir Carrots, and Aryn wants to check in on the big cat, so we two head off to the Arisek stockyards. The townsfolk are moving around with their normal slightly depressed shuffles, and we arrive at the yard.

We notice the damage caused by the cat's original rampage is still very visible. We're keeping an eye out for Rictavio or anyone else we know; we do notice a few observing ravens scattered along the street, and one particular bird shadows us along the road the entire way.

The stockyard is already busy. Charity immediately heads to Sir Carrots' stable. She immediately notices he's been freshly brushed, his water is fresh, and there's a little collection of carrots right there. She's happy about the care being shown to him and feels much better about leaving him alone for a while.

Meanwhile, Aryn checks in on the barn-like building we left the sabre-tooth in. The door is still locked and everything looks the same as when we left. Aryn unlocks the door, calls "good morning, kitty!" and slowly eases open the door. However, the cat is missing. The water bucket is there, and Aryn notices a slip of paper in the middle of the room where we left the cat. She glances at the note but can't read it.

She rushes back out and meets Charity. We huddle together and look at the note. It's in Sylvan, which Charity can read.

```
Thank you for taking such good care of my friend. I have reciprocated as much as I can. However I think we need to talk.

R.V.R.
```

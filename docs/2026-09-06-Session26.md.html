Session 26

2026 09 06

We start this session with a split party. Aryn and Charity are at the stockyard looking at a note from Rictavio; Banhammer and Thalion started their day at the Bluewater but we haven't checked in on them for a while.

We head back to the pub and catch the boys up on what's happened. We're not sure what to do next; if we can't think of anything fun in town, we can always go explore to the south.

Danica says it's unusual that Ric hasn't been in yet today, and we volunteer to take Blinsky's lunch over. It's the day after the festival so it's a quiet walk. Blinsky's shop is open for business and he seems happy enough; he apologises to Aryn for not having her order ready yet.

We ask if he's seen Rictavio today; he seems puzzled by the question since Ric isn't here often. It's been weeks since he saw Ric last, though he does check in periodically to see Piccolo.

Blinsky thinks Isaac is probably doing cleanup today. He has no news about the church other than it was attacked, and doesn't know anything specific about the mysterious unknown regions south of the town.

Blinsky asks Aryn for help. He's missing some rare components for constructing her doll.

He tells is that in the early days of Borovia's transformation into Strahd's prison, there was a war between Strahd's faction and a rebel group. They operated from a fortified villa, Argynvostholt, and Blinsky believes the ruins of that villa will still contain fine silks from the times before.

The villa is not too far from the road but is seldom visited, because it's thought dangerous, but Blinsky thinks we would be up to it. He tells us how to get there.

We head for the Church of St Andral to see if there's anything to the rumours that the theft was an inside job. As we approach the crowds thin out more than we'd expect; we suspect that fear of somehow earning Strahd's disfavour means people are actively avoiding the church and even the local area.

There's a single parishioner in the church, praying quietly to themselves. For now, we don't see the priest or anyone else. We spend a few minutes repairing pews, or at least Thalion applies his smith's competency to repair pews while the rest of us supervise.

Father Lucian appears and thanks us for our service. Charity asks him about the theft of the relic, and he relays that he isn't sure when exactly the theft happened -- there was no sign of a break-in or anything that could put a date on the theft, but the first time he realised they were gone was the attack where Ireena was taken.

We ask to talk to the lads who help out. Miliboj is outside digging graves, but the altar boy, Yeska, is around and Lucian calls him out from the church office.

Yeska is an orphan and his age is hard to determine. As Lucian tells Yeska that we'd like to talk about the relics, and Thalion immediately clocks that Yeska is keeping some kind of secret.

We start with asking Yeska about his role and tasks at the church. He's pretty vague about it -- replacing candles, filling the font, etc. We ask of any of those duties lead Yeska in or around the crypt entrance. He says not really and the entrance is always locked.

We ask if anyone has talked to him about the crypt or the relics, outside of Fr Lucian. He says that Lucian had recently told him how there was something in the crypt that kept the church safe. We ask if he shared that good news with anyone else, and he remembers speaking to Miliboj about it. This was news to Fr Lucian, and to Yeska it seemed like M didn't know before that.

We decide we're good with Yeska, but we notice that Fr Lucien is thinking about Milivoj. We propose talking to M next, and Lucien breaks through he reverie and agrees. He says we should go outside to talk to him, because he's a bit temperamental. The group shares a look and we all troop outside, minus Lucien who wants to go talk to Y a bit more.

M is outside, not digging but picking up small rocks and batting them over the churchyard fence with his shovel. We loom a bit as we ask for a chat.

"We were just talking to Yeska about the church's holy relic protections. Did you know about the relics?"

He scoffs. "That kid can't keep his mouth shut, he was telling me about it minutes after he heard from Fr Lucien."

We keep talking and he admits that he does go into the crypt occasionally as part of his duties, mostly interring the town's notables (and at those times the mourners don't enter the crypt).

He says he doesn't get out much as a poor orphan with younger siblings to care for, but he definitely hasn't discussed the crypt with anyone. Thalion is pretty confident that this is a lie and we press further.

He does recall one night after a church funeral, when the local coffin maker, Henrik van der Voort, who offered him a warm meal at an inn. M remembers the conversation might have included some discussion of the crypt and its contents. We ask and M thinks Henrik wasn't that pushy but he did seem pretty interested in the crypt.

Thalion has Milivoj's number by now and can tell that we're not getting the full story. Thalion speaks up. "Look here's the deal. We're looking for the relic, we want to go get it, and if we can get it we can put it back and we can all stop talking about it."

M looks at Thalion's stern face, then up at the head of Banhammer's huge maul, formed by a impressively twisted up battleaxe. His confession spills out of him.

Henrik asked M to steal the relics, and he did. He used his shovel to pry up the slabs of the church floor to get access to the crypt, took the relics and delivered them to Henrik, and in return received food and coin.

We ask for details about Henrik and get his address. It seems like M is much more concerned with the loss of trust from Fr Lucien rather than any kind of retribution from Henrik.

We ask M if he wants to go see Fr Lucien right now and tell him together. He seems terrified of that. We decide that we'll give him some time to confess to Fr L while we go chase Henrik.

We wrap up with M and head back to the church. Charity sees a raven who croaks just as the Strahd booty-call stone bips. Charity pulls out the stone.

A little birdie told me you like a man with muscles, so I hope you enjoy my gift. (eggplant)(waterspash)(peach)

Charity responds with (cat)(desert).

We look around expecting something to happen but nothing is out of the ordinary. After a minute we shrug and head back through the church, telling Lucien that we're on the trail and will catch up with him later. He calls a blessing after us as we abscond.

As we head out of the church district we come across a crowd in the road. Thalion peers over heads but it's hard to make out what's going on. We elbow in a bit and as we work in, we start to hear Gastonovich's voice, and we see he's lugging a pair of direwolf bodies, one over each shoulder.

We see his eyes flicker and his posture and attitude suddenly changes. He drops the wolves to the ground, and in moments the wolf bodies stir and stand up.

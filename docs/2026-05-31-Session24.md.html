So we're in a boat with a wet kid and a confused fisherman, looking toward the shore where Strahd (Gigachad version) is standing.

He has a pack of wolves around him, sitting calmly waiting. There's not much else to do but paddle back to the shore. We pull the boat up onto the shore.

Charity is pretty grumpy about the situation and goes to confront Strahd for attempting murder on this child who has done nothing to him.

At first he tries to deflect by saying he just starts things and the eventual effects are up to people like the fisherman, but Charity calls him a bully and the argument eventually devolves into Strahd claiming that Might Makes Right, and Charity calling him a Nigel No-friends. We're all aware that he could probably kill us all right now, but he seems to like the idea of toying with people who are willing to oppose him.

Banhammer hoists his hammer in a casually threatening way, and calls "what business are we going to have here?"

Strahd assesses Arabelle, and it occurs to Banhammer that he is at least considering letting us pass. BH scoops up Arabelle and starts walking past Strahd.

The fisherman is still a little dazed or maybe (based on the empty wineskins in his boat) he's just drunk. Charity calls out to the fisherman, inviting him to come back to the town. He's too zonked and she has to walk back to him and drag him by the collar, deftly dodging a projection of vomit.

Strahd laughs at this but lets us all march past.

Halfway back to Vallaki we run into Arabelle's mother, who is incredibly grateful for us saving her daughter and promises her support if we ever need it. She hands us a tarot-card-like token that she says will let other Vistani know that we're a friend, and can be exchanged for a favour.

BH: Acquires a Vistani favour token

When we get back to the town and the Bluewater it's mid afternoon; it's still festival day but most of the outside stalls have closed up and the energy has died out a little. Inside the pub it's still cracking on in a relaxed way. We chill for a few hours, waiting for our late night mysterious meeting.

During that afternoon we hear more about Isaac, the town's 2IC. He's known to be cruel and there's plenty of gossip about him doing abusive bullying in various situations. He's the Baron/Burgermeister's headkicker and that role protects him from much of the consequences of his own psychotic actions. He grew up an orphan and found his way into the Baron's favour.

We also hear something about the bones of St Andral. The rough opinion is that the theft is probably connected to Fr Lucian or one of the two boys associated with the church. We don't think we've met both boys so we resolve to go talk to the church staff tomorrow.

Eventually Danica chases the last non-resident out of the bar. She invites us to sit around the fire as long as we like and heads off to bed herself. It's time to go to our late night meeting.

It's at a crossroads, which are abandoned and all the nearby houses are shuttered and dark. We stand around for a minute and Charity decides to sneak off and hide behind some front porch stairs, just in case. Banhammer stands in the middle of the road, a small steam whistle tootling gently.

A few minutes later someone clears their throat right next to Charity. She jumps a bit. This dark figure had been hiding in the same spot, and Charity hadn't noticed. He steps out and tells us:

"It's good to know you weren't followed. The Lady likes her affairs to be discreet. The Lady honours you to even allow you an audience with her."

He strides off, expecting us to follow, and we do. Banhammer tries to convey that he's not following deferentially but because he has business in this direction. We follow him down a poorly-lit side street and he abruptly stops and scans the area, then ducks off the road and heads across the dirt and grass area, leading us to the rear entrance of a large building.

The place looks shuttered, but we can just make out a very faint outline of light around some of the windows. This dilapidated mansion seems disgusted with itself -- a slouching roof, sagging gables, and as we observe, an audible creaking groan.

Our guide pauses again and scans around looking for anyone else in the area. It's empty, and he reaches for the door and opens it. "She will be completing her evening address. Please wait in the servant quarters to your right until someone summons you."

We step into the house and through to the servants quarters. There are four folk dozing on the four beds in this room.

Through an open door to the south we hear talking.

"The old order weakens, we need new leaders with the power to embrace our lord! Soon Vallaki will be freed from its foolishness!"

"And now, let us offer out gratitude to our Price of Darkness!"

Thalion identifies a circle of chalk on the floor and the people in the room to the south shuffle into a circle around it. They all start chanting, and there's an air of theatricality to it.

A tiny horned figure materialises in a puff of smoke, grins, and scatters a few electrum coins onto the floor, and disappear again. "Behold!" declares the woman, as her followers scrabble to scoop up the coins.

The woman running the ceremony has had her back to us this whole time and is shaking people hands as they leave. Once the room is empty, she calls out without turning around "You can come in now, everyone else has left." She moves across the parlor to the couches arranged around the fire. Above the fire is a portrait of a nobleman, and other portraits hang on the other walls.

The furniture seems worn, the tableware is chipped and needs a polish, and It's clear to Banhammer that the mantle's large portrait looks a lot like the two tavern noble bretheren, one of whom invited us here.

A minute later our original guide, Earnst, enters the room via the door the other cultists left through.

She silently collects a goblet and wine and goes through the motions of pouring herself some. Charity reads quite a bit from this action of doing it herself and not offering any to her guests.

"I hear you have had an encounter with our erstwhile Burgermeister, and got to experience his clumsy inadequacies first hand. It's convenient that you got to see not only the quality of our Bm but the people he surrounds himself with." While we talk, the same imp who materialised in the summoning circle materialises on her shoulder. We learn his name is Majesto. She's playing it very snooty and dismissive of both us and the people in her own group (including )

"I've had Earnst watching you while we released our tiger. And Blinsky didn't deserve what happened to him." She outlines a number of other issues and events around town, each time focussing on the Burgermeister's incompetent handling of the situation.

Charity announces that she will have some after all, collects a goblet from the same place, pours a little wine from the same bottle, and then sets the goblet down without sipping any.

Charity enquires after any entertaining diversions planned for this festival season. She almost scoffs about how easy it's been to raise a loyal-enough group of commoners to plan her next event -- dancing around the topic of her proposed governmental overthrow -- but she also declares that she wants to recruit some more important, impactful folk to get the important parts done. This seems to be her motivation for talking to us.

"So it's an alliance you propose," Charity suggests. She doesn't like this framing and says it's more of a exchange of services with a limited time delivery.

Aryn is getting a bit tired of the repartee and asks "So, what exactly is it you want us to do?"

"The biggest hurdle is Isaac. Barely even a man -- more a monster." She outlines his reputation and recent history, and the unusual strength that makes him a challenge. She adds more detail to his back story, describing how he once had a happy family in Vallaki with parents, uncle, and sister. Then one morning they went fishing on Lake Zarovitch, and a dire wolf attacked and tore off Issac's arm. His father carried him into town, while uncle defended against the wolf and sister ran off into the woods, never to be seen again.

His parents succumbed to their grief and Issac was left alone. His life became a cruel trial, and he started killing the young townsfolk who teased him. The Baron forgave him and took him into his home.

We know that when we saw Issac at the festival was using two perfectly functional arms, and her voice drops to a whisper as she describes that some say Issac's arm is the result of a deal that he or the Burghermeister made with a devil. This theory is supported by weird stuff about the Burghermeister's house -- lights, smells, etc that are suspicious.

Most of Issac's victims die, but the survivors have various stories about him summoning fire, or turning into a great white dog, or becoming a demon himself.

She concludes that Issac is loyal, powerful, and feared, and so is the Baron's primary defence. Removing him would clear the way for the Lady's faction to take over. She reminds us of the general unhappiness and tension we saw during the festival.

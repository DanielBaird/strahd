Session 29

2026 11 01

So we just got a mission to clear out some hags. We've been advised that they're based in the windmill that we have a deed to. It's going to be a long trip so we decide we'll overnight back in town and then set out tomorrow.

We have no issues at the gate or getting back to the Bluewater. We have a chill evening, until there's a bit of a scuffle between Gastonovich supporters and the growing group of people who say he was always an asshole.

Drunk Charity fails to calm the excitement, and soon Irwin bursts into the room from the kitchen, flanked by Danica and her crossbow, and yell for everyone to leave. The group spend a second or two considering if they could keep brawling, and in the quiet we can hear a faint hiss of steam and the creaking groan of Banhammer's chair as he stands up to face the crowd. They all collectively decide they should pursue their interests in other venues, and exit the inn.

Thalion turns in, wisely, but Aryn and Charity continue to get hammered. There's dancing, almost-falling, and Charity challenges Banhammer, who is a poison-resistant construct, to keep up.

We end up playing Two Truths and a Lie. Charity goes first:

    1. My family had. farmer killed because he wouldn't sell his farm to the family trust
    1. My first kiss was with a Crown Prince
    1. The dancing master who taught me courtly dancing was sent to prison soon after

Aryn and Banhammer both correctly guess that #2 is the lie -- Charity admits that it was actually with a stableboy.

Banhammer goes next:

    1. I once was a man
    1. I once rebuild myself from separate parts
    1. Pretended to be a tree for seven months, to catch a predator

Aryn guesses #2 as the lie, and Charity guesses #3. We're both wrong.

Aryn gives us her three:

    1. I can play ten instruments
    1. My ancestor invented the bagpipes
    1. My home village is famous for only producing bards

Charity chooses #3, Banhammer chooses #1 and is right. Charity slowly stands to do her penalty drink, ceremonially toasting the empty bar.

Meanwhile, Thalion, who has fallen asleep to the distant sounds of carousing, wakens to a scratching at the window. He sees Henrik, the coffin maker, staring in through the first-floor window. Thalion rolls out of bed and vaults over the balcony down into the taproom where the rest of us are. He reaches out to Charity and some of her cloud of drunkenness falls away.

We listen to Henrik break in to our room through the window and rummaging around in the room. Is he looking for the relic bones?

Charity yells out "I've got your relic right here ya bastard!" as she pulls her bow out. We hear Henrik stop searching and he busts through the door, emerging into the second floor of the taproom. He's clearly been turned into a vampire of some sort.

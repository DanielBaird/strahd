Session 27

2026 09 20

We see his eyes flicker and his posture and attitude suddenly changes. He drops the wolves to the ground, and in moments the wolf bodies stir and stand up.

So we're shaping up Possessed Gastonovich and his two wolf buddies.

Charity, still on the lookout for Strahd's fun little promised gift, is first on the draw and lands a cracker shot against a wolf. The wolf lunges at her, foolishly ignoring Banhammer who mauls it into a bloody pulp. Banhammer runs over to land another hit at the second wolf.

Meanwhile Gastonovich shakes off Aryn's cutting insult as he unslings his hunting gun and blasts a slug at her.

Thalion summons a swirl of spirit guardians and runs his spirit tornado into G and the remaining wolf.

Flocks of ravens swoop in and attack Charity and the nearby commoners, who initially were fleeing but some stayed when they saw how quickly we downed the first wolf. She flails around and catches a couple, but another group swoops down to attack G.

Banhammer fells the second wolf as Aryn sends evil whispers to G, who seems to shake it off and pulls his big Bowie knife out and flashes it around, catching a couple of ravens.

Thalion unfolds a spiritual weapon out of space and it swoops around behind G and attacks, on top of the swirl of spirits still orbiting him.

G is distracted enough that Aryn's next insult impacts him emotionally, followed by a maul which impacts him quite physically, earning Banhammer a few stabs from G's knife.

Charity, who's wolf has reanimated again, takes a different tack, stepping away from the wolf and shooting at Gastonovich. Her shot lands but the wolf pulls her to the ground with it's teeth sunk into her calf.

Banhammer activates a fire rune and smashes his burning maul into G, who cops the hit hard and replies with a few knife stabs, but doesn't notice the spirit wielded forge hammer that delivers a final strike, cracking his skull.

The wolves fall down dead properly, and the ravens fly off. We check on the downed raven friendlies, but there are no apparent were-ravens taken out.

A few guards are hurrying down the road, with varying levels of enthusiasm. Thalion rifles through G's body and collects a couple of special items before the guards arrive.

Charity acquires the Topaz Annihilator

Topaz Annihilator

Ranged weapon (firearm), legendary (requires attunement)

This magic ranged weapon resembles a musket, but in lieu of any ammunition, it holds a glowing yellow scale from a topaz dragon in its heart.

The weapon has a normal range of 100 feet and a long range of 300 feet, and it has the two-handed property. It deals 2d6 necrotic damage on a hit. If this damage reduces a creature or object to 0 hit points, the target is reduced to dust. A creature reduced to dust can be restored to life only by a true resurrection or wish spell.

While the weapon is on your person, you can use an action to cast the disintegrate spell (save DC 18). Once this property is used, it can't be used again.

The person who ran to get the guards appears to be a Gastonovich supporter, but they're pretty quiet when they get to the pile of bodies around us. The guards themselves are pretty neutral until one of the surviving townsfolk tells them the full story, which from their point of view was that Gastonovich came into town with two direwolves, talked up a crowd, and then brought the wolves to life to attack everyone, until we arrived and sorted it out.

The guards decide that we're heroes not troublemakers, and they suggest that we could maybe get started on the cleanup just like we were deputised as town guards to do the cleanup after the sabretooth cat incident a couple of days back. Aryn convinces them that it would look great on the guard captain's report if they took charge of that themselves, and we watch the guard captain realise that as the report writer he could claim that he was in fact the main saviour of the town.

We inspect our wounds and decide to head back to the inn rather than go straight to Henrik the Gravediggers house. It's even quieter on the streets as word of the fight has spread.

Danica has a fresh scratch on her face. Charity notices this and tells her that we really appreciate everything she's done to make the group feel safe and welcome in town. We share a look of acknowledgement, and then she hands over a folded note.

Charity glances at it as she moves to our table, fearing a sequence of emojis, but it's in Common with a signature she doesn't recognise. We read it at the table.

You are the ones responsible for saving my daughter. If you are interested in reward for your deeds, seek me out at the Vistani encampment.

- Luvash

After lunch we head out for Henrik's house.
